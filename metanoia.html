METANOIA - GENUFLECTION

"Μετάνοια" in Greek is a word with two meanings. One of them comes from 'μετά + νοῦς', which means literally change of mind; in Christian culture, it is the realisation and admission of wrongdoing and the change of way of life. The other meaning of the word refers to genuflection as an act. The movement one would do to return to oneself, the internal change of direction, the rebirth, and the transformation from old to new.
