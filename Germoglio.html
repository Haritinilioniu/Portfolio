GERMOGLIO - SPROUT

This piece is a meditation of genesis, the balance of fragility and strength. The form emerges from the stone just as a plant grows from the ground: slowly, with silent strength, toward the light. The body, curved and welcoming, is simultaneously seed and womb[cite: 1]. The pronounced belly evokes maternity and waiting, but also the generative power of nature[cite: 1].
Material
White Marble (Carrara)
Year
2024-2025
Course
