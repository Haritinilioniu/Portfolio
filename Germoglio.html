GERMOGLIO - SPROUT

This piece is a meditation of genesis, the balance of fragility and strength. The form emerges from the stone just as a plant grows from the ground: slowly, with silent strength, toward the light. The body, curved and welcoming, is simultaneously seed and womb. The pronounced belly evokes maternity and waiting, but also the generative power of nature.
Material White Marble (Carrara) Year 2024-2025 Course Tecniche Del Marmo e Pietre Dure At Accademia di Belle Arti di Carrara
